Road choice study

A two-stage survey of women. Form A collects, in their own words, what about a road changes their choice to use it. Form B asks a different group to rank the most-mentioned parameters by best-worst scaling.

Form A: elicitationOpen questions and free lists, in two blocks. About 6 minutes.Open Form B: best-worst rankingSets of 4 parameters, most and least. About 8 minutes.
